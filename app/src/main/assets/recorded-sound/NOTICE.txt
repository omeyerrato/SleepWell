SleepWell changes: evaluation-time classifiers exported to ONNX, separate Java
audio frontends, experimental coarse selection policy. No weight retraining.
Authors do not endorse SleepWell. Original 527-class AudioSet mapping preserved.
Original checkpoint and exported model hashes, source revisions, full notices,
export recipe and retained missed examples are in the public SleepWell source:
third_party/supplementary-sound/

Outputs are experimental uncalibrated sound estimates. A missed or uncertain
window is not evidence that laughter or instruments were absent. This feature
does not replace the automatic snoring/speech/cough/cry classifier or diagnose
sleep or medical conditions.
