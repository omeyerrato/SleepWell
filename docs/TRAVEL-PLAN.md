# Travel plan and user-chosen daylight reminders

This is a travel clock/plan and actual local-reminder candidate. It does **not** implement, prescribe, or claim jet-lag prevention. The supplied board 09 screen 27 informs the cream layout, grouped zone cards, departure row and paired orange/lilac clocks. Experimental sleep flashes and smart-light treatment are not represented by working toggles.

Implemented source:

- Search all installed IANA identifiers by city/identifier; choose separate saved home and destination zones. Zone cards show actual current UTC offsets. Clock hands, digital times, local dates and the current difference refresh while the page is visible. A separate departure-day difference accounts for intervening DST changes.
- Choose a real calendar departure date, destination-local reminder time and one to fourteen consecutive reminder days. Settings remain a draft until Save. Open, cancel, recreation, storage errors and concurrent revision changes preserve existing saved data.
- Explicit optional daylight notifications use the time **the user chooses**, beginning on the departure date interpreted in the destination zone. No clinical phase calculation is performed. The home zone is never inferred from the sign of an offset, and a date-line difference is not interpreted as a flight direction.
- A real AlarmManager inexact wake-up request schedules the next occurrence. Its immutable explicit private PendingIntent is unique to the saved revision and occurrence. Edits and disabling cancel the old request and visible notification. Duplicate, stale, early and consumed callbacks cannot show a reminder.
- Delivery consumes its generation before posting a silent notification and queues the next future day. Cues more than one hour late are skipped, avoiding a daylight prompt many hours after its chosen time. Missed times are not backfilled. Permission/channel denial and scheduling failures have user-facing recovery states.
- Protected boot, package-update and clock/time-zone broadcasts restore future saved requests. Opening the saved plan also reconciles requests. Changing the phone's zone does not rewrite either user-selected IANA zone. Spring gaps resolve forward to a valid local time; fall overlaps use the earlier occurrence once.

Platform limits: Android may delay inexact delivery, suppress notifications, or stop background behavior after a force-stop. Reopen the app to reconcile. A process death between private state and AlarmManager/notification calls can skip a request/cue; those APIs do not provide a shared transaction. Requests are repaired on reopening or the listed system broadcasts. No exact-alarm privilege, microphone, flashlight, vibration, media playback, cloud account, or wearable is required. Actual OS delivery, reboot and long-term device behavior remain unverified until controlled device/emulator evidence exists.

`TravelPlanTest` covers IANA lookup, actual DST/fractional/date-line offsets, local-calendar schedules, spring/fall resolution, finite periods and clock rollback. `TravelReminderPolicyTest` covers generation/deduplication/early/late fences. `TravelPlanFlowTest` is a CI-only native configuration/storage flow with an injected clock, scheduler and notification output. It also exercises the actual receiver engine's consumption, rearming, stale callback rejection, clock rollback, failure/retry and disable behavior. These tests do not activate real OS reminders or physical outputs. Expected screenshots: `travel-plan`, `travel-zones`, `travel-date`, `travel-reminders`, `travel-reopened`, `travel-error`, and `travel-disabled`.

Research boundary: [Sleep as Android's jet-lag chapter](https://www.sleep.urbandroid.org/docs/sleep/jetlag.html) describes a sleep-flash intervention. That algorithm and hardware timing have not been implemented or validated here. The [CDC Yellow Book chapter](https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/travel-air-sea/jet-lag-disorder.html) explains that appropriate light timing depends on circadian phase, direction and time zones crossed, and points to individualized planning. A fixed default reminder time is not a personalized treatment. This app therefore offers explicit user-selected reminders rather than silently presenting an experimental flashing algorithm as proven prevention.
