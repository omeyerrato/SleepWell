# Sleep rhythm: descriptive timing, not a chronotype diagnosis

`SleepRhythmActivity` follows board 09's left screen: cream background, Inter typography, large bedtime-range metric, dated purple bedtime markers with a saved-goal line, work/free midpoint comparison and recorded-night progress. The extra Workday schedule route has direct switches, Save, cancel/back, saved-state restoration and explicit storage errors. Opening either route does not persist defaults.

Analytics use the shared effective `RecordEntry` coverage and `RecordTrends` sleep-day grouping. Closed periods must contain at least three hours of effective coverage and have no `nap` tag, matching the existing History main-period convention. This is a display heuristic, not a clinical threshold. Each sleep-day chooses its longest eligible period, with stable update/id tie-breaking. Physically duplicated intervals already claimed by an earlier grouped day do not add another night. Missing, future, in-progress and tagged-nap records are excluded. Manual periods contribute reported timing and remain identified as user-reported; only distinct selected periods with source `tracked` count toward phone-recorded history.

The chart shows the last seven *available* main nights with their actual dates. Missing days are not fabricated. Marker capsules are visual markers at actual bedtime dots, not uncertainty intervals. The bedtime is the first effective covered instant after edge trims; midpoint is halfway through elapsed time between the first and last effective covered instants, converted to that period's recorded time zone. DST transitions therefore use actual elapsed time. Older records with a missing or invalid stored zone follow RecordEntry and use the phone current zone; those timings have that inherited limitation. Internal pauses and awake periods reduce coverage eligibility but do not turn the halfway point into a sleep-stage estimate. Bedtime variability is the length of the smallest containing arc on a 24-hour clock. Circular means and shortest clock-time differences handle midnight; opposite/undefined means remain unavailable.

Workdays are explicitly chosen by the user and classify a night by its local **wake calendar weekday**, independently of History's configurable sleep-day cutoff. The descriptive social-jetlag value compares work/free circular period-midpoint means for the displayed nights, equally weighted per night. Both groups must exist and have representative means; otherwise the UI asks for the missing nights. No workweek or free-day pattern is silently inferred, and a fixed weekly schedule is not presented as a shift-work model.

The 30-night target is history progress from the design and Sleep as Android's display requirement. Reaching it does **not** assign a clinical chronotype, population percentile, owl/lark category or a medical cutoff. SleepWell does not have the structured alarm-free sleep-onset, recovery-sleep and schedule information used by the Munich ChronoType Questionnaire. It does not copy that questionnaire or its licensed materials.

Primary-source boundary: [the original social-jetlag paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16687322/) discusses differences between work and free-day timing; [the MCTQ developer's documentation](https://www.thewep.org/documentations/mctq/item/mctq) describes free-day midsleep corrected for recovery sleep and requests permission for questionnaire use. [Sleep as Android's rhythm documentation](https://www.sleep.urbandroid.org/docs/sleep/chrono_jetlag.html) describes its own clustering and 30-day chronotype display, which are not claimed as implemented here.

`RhythmStatsTest` covers circular ranges/means, missing groups, actual schedule changes, effective manual/awake edits, DST folds, nap/open/future exclusions, duplicate sleep-days/physical intervals, and evidence beyond the visible seven nights. `SleepRhythmFlowTest` is an explicit emulator-CI-only flow with real private SQLite timing fixtures and a private settings file. It verifies actual workday switches, draft recreation/cancel, Save/reopen, changed analytics, unchanged fixture history and global preferences, and an empty database. It does not run a service, sensor, alarm or audio output. Expected captures: `rhythm-seven-nights`, `rhythm-workdays`, `rhythm-recreated`, `rhythm-reopened`, `rhythm-empty`.
