# Additional offline sound estimates — candidate evaluation

The already bundled YAMNet mobile model exports Cough42, Throat clearing43, Baby cry20 and Laughter13–18. This candidate extends the existing offline PCM classifier and SQLite event path; it does not infer categories from amplitude, download a new model, retrospectively relabel existing rows, change schema, or enable any output. SnoreCuePolicy still accepts only snoring; cough, crying and laughter cannot allocate or emit cues. No new permission, service or network call is added.

Each additional category needs an uncalibrated score at least0.65, a margin at least0.15 against every unrelated model label, and two consecutive matching0.975second windows before persistence. The ten-window event bound,3600metadata-event/night budget, optional WAV budget and inference failure fallback are unchanged. Laughter uses the maximum of its parent13 and exported children14–18, not a sum of correlated scores. Baby crying requires the specific20 label; its Crying19 ancestor may coexist but cannot supply infant evidence, and Whimper21 still competes. Cough includes its exported Throat clearing43 child and the user-facing label therefore explicitly reads Cough / throat clear estimate. Other respiratory sounds still compete. Snoring and Speech keep their existing score/margin rules.

These are conservative engineering defaults, not thresholds calibrated on bedrooms or medical recordings. The two-window gate can miss short coughs and brief sounds. A sound may come from television, another person or an animal; the model cannot identify the sleeper, diagnose an illness, determine an infant's condition, or establish safety. No per-class sensitivity setting is added because this small fixture set cannot justify such calibration. Old event categories and independent recording/event stars remain unchanged.

Primary label/ontology references: [official YAMNet521-class map](https://github.com/tensorflow/models/blob/master/research/audioset/yamnet/yamnet_class_map.csv), [Cough including Throat clearing](https://research.google.com/audioset/ontology/cough_1.html), [Laughter and children](https://research.google.com/audioset/ontology/laughter.html), [Crying with Baby cry child](https://research.google.com/audioset/ontology/crying_sobbing.html), [Baby cry](https://research.google.com/audioset/ontology/baby_cry_infant_cry.html). No ontology JSON is bundled or copied.

## Required acceptance evidence

Four additional uploader-published CC0 audio previews are converted to PCM16/mono16000Hz, with a first30second maximum selected before initial inference (the later full-preview laughter reevaluation is explained below). There is no gain normalization, tiling, resynthesis or selection of successful windows. Files ship only in the test APK. Existing two snore, solo speech and crowd recordings remain cross-class negative/regression fixtures.

SoundCategoryFixtureEvaluationTest has one CI-only emulator test. It feeds these actual PCM recordings in1024sample chunks through the production SoundAnalysisStream, real CPU model, unchanged framing/group lifecycle and Store, then reopens SQLite and checks persisted categories/durations/model identity. It writes every window's new-class scores, top8labels, selected category and all persisted events to analysis-category-fixture-evaluation.json before the positive-class acceptance assertions. Acceptance requires a grouped stored cough across the two independent cough recordings, grouped baby crying and laughter from their recordings, no new-category events on the old snore/speech recordings, and retained positive snore/solo speech. Analysis-only fixtures must not create clips. Native execution, per-fixture results and UI filter/screenshots are pending; the new categories are not yet verified features.

Seven pure policy tests cover compatible family labels/maximum, ancestor-only rejection, unrelated/respiratory competitors, unchanged score/margin gates, isolated-window rejection, non-snore cue rejection and exact estimate labels.
