# Rain-on-glass asset

- Saved asset: `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/night_dial_rain.png`.
- Created: 2026-10-30 with the built-in imagegen tool.
- Purpose: source-specific artwork on Tonight's Rain sound card. Controls and copy remain native.

- The existing purple bedroom asset was inspected and retained for compatibility; it is not used by the new Rain card.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: standalone in-app sound artwork for SleepWell Night Dial, no interface. Make a landscape 3:2 photographic macro view of real rain droplets and irregular flowing rivulets on a dark glass window at night. Entirely neutral monochrome black and charcoal with restrained silver reflections. Sharp varied droplets in foreground, blurred dark outdoor silhouettes behind the wet glass, organic variation, subtle realistic depth, fine photographic texture. Rich tactile image with most pixels dark; no bright sky, no bright bokeh balls, no plants, no people, no bed, no lamp, no illustration, no purple cast, no sepia, no text, no labels, no phone frame, no controls, no logo, no borders. Bottom quarter slightly darker naturally so native white text can be placed over it later. This is a bitmap content asset, not a screen mockup.
