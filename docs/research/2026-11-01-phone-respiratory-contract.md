## Actual input and persistence

`SonarDsp` processes actual mono 48 kHz, 40 ms capture frames against the configured 18.5–20 kHz FMCW chirp. With periodicity enabled, it passes 23 complex reflection bins only on qualified frames. `SonarNight` sends those transient observations to `EchoPeriodicity`; they are not an archived I/Q recording. Its recent movement points omit I/Q. `EchoPeriods` persists only each complete minute's start/end, nullable rate, correlation and quality. The analyzer averages five frames to 5 Hz, projects each bin onto a principal axis, detrends and checks full/half-window autocorrelation agreement. It does not retain the projected waveform or individual cycle times.

Therefore an existing saved minute cannot recover individual breaths, a ten-second breathing interruption, waveform amplitude reduction, respiratory effort, or a stage model's waveform/context. Sparse sound-event clips are a separate opted-in recording path and do not reconstruct continuous input. Missing/conflicting echoes remain unresolved; periodic movement can have nonrespiratory sources. Current quality/gap handling supplies no trained mapping from a reflection to breathing or apnea. No reference-supported capture/DSP correction was identified in this audit, so none was made.
